About BASMIR

BASMIR Lab advances accurate, explainable, and secure computing for biomedical discovery, clinical decision support, retinal screening, and resilient digital infrastructure.

Our work connects biomedical AI with cybersecurity, privacy-preserving learning, multimodal medical imaging, cloud systems, and post-quantum security.

We organize collaborative research around focused tracks while encouraging cross-track projects that move from data and models to validated, useful systems.

Research tracks

Track 1

Medical Imaging and Multimodal Biomedical AI

Medical image understanding, 3D point clouds, multimodal large language models, transformers, and decision support for biomedical applications.

Track 2

Trustworthy and Privacy-Preserving AI

Federated learning, privacy, robustness, explainability, data-efficient learning, and reliable AI for sensitive health data.

Track 3

AI-Driven Cybersecurity and Secure Healthcare Systems

Intrusion detection, malware analysis, blockchain security, secure healthcare infrastructure, and AI for resilient digital systems.

Track 4

Post-Quantum Cryptography and Embedded Systems Security

Post-quantum cryptography, secure boot, side-channel and fault-attack resilience, embedded systems, IoT, and autonomous platforms.

Track 5

Retinal AI and Computational Ophthalmology

Retinal imaging, diabetic retinopathy, retinopathy of prematurity, screening, triage, clinical validation, and explainable ophthalmic AI.
