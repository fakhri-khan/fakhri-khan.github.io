Research philosophy

From rigorous methods to systems people can trust.

The common thread is trust: trust in data, trust in models, trust in infrastructure, and trust in the decisions these systems support.

Projects combine machine learning, distributed systems, cybersecurity, data engineering, and applied evaluation. The work is interdisciplinary by design and shaped by practical constraints.
