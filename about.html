About

Research with a practical sense of purpose.

Dr. Fakhri Alam Khan is an Associate Professor at King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals in Dhahran, Saudi Arabia.

I work at the intersection of information security, trustworthy AI, healthcare analytics, cloud provenance, and business informatics.

My academic path includes a Ph.D. with distinction from the University of Vienna, postdoctoral research in Austria and Korea, and academic leadership across teaching, program development, supervision, and funded research.

My research goal is to build computing systems that are secure, explainable, and useful beyond the laboratory—especially where technology touches health, public infrastructure, and organizational decision-making.
