About

An academic career built around useful research.

I serve as an Associate Professor at King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals. My work combines rigorous computing research with practical problems in healthcare, secure infrastructure, cloud systems, and data-driven decision-making.

My academic path includes a Ph.D. with distinction from the University of Vienna, postdoctoral research in Austria and Korea, and academic leadership across teaching, program development, supervision, and funded research.
